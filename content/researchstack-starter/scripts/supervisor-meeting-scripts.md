# Supervisor Meeting Scripts

## 15-Minute Meeting Agenda

1. Introduce yourself
2. Explain your interest and availability
3. Ask what projects are active or realistic
4. Clarify approval/governance requirements
5. Clarify your role
6. Clarify output and timeline
7. Agree next action

## Introduction Script

Thank you for meeting with me. I am a [year level] medical student at [University], and I am interested in [specialty/topic]. I am hoping to get involved in a realistic research, audit, QI, or case report project where I can contribute consistently and learn the process properly.

At the moment, I can commit around [time] per week. I am comfortable helping with literature searches, data extraction, reference management, drafting, and abstract preparation.

## Questions To Ask

Project:

- What projects are currently active in the team?
- Are there any projects suitable for a medical student?
- Is the question already defined?
- What is the expected timeline?

Approvals:

- Is this research, audit, QI, evaluation, or case report?
- What approvals or governance steps are required?
- Has anything already been submitted or approved?
- Can a student access the data?

Role:

- What would my first task be?
- How often should I update you?
- Who else is involved?
- What skills should I learn before starting?

Output:

- Are we aiming for a poster, abstract, presentation, or manuscript?
- Is there a target conference or journal?
- How will contribution/authorship be decided?

## End-of-Meeting Close

Thank you, that sounds helpful. To make sure I understood correctly, my next step is to [next action] by [date]. I will update [person] once that is done, and we can then decide the next stage.
