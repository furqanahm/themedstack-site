# Output and Publication Roadmap

Research helps your CV when it becomes clear evidence of contribution.

The aim is not only "publication." Outputs can build towards publication.

## Output Ladder

### Level 1: Project Evidence

- project brief
- literature matrix
- data extraction sheet
- audit criteria table
- ethics/governance submission support
- meeting notes
- contribution log

### Level 2: Local Output

- department presentation
- hospital audit meeting
- student society presentation
- teaching session
- poster draft

### Level 3: Conference Output

- abstract
- poster
- oral presentation
- conference proceedings
- prize nomination

### Level 4: Publication Output

- case report
- audit/QI manuscript
- review article
- original research paper
- letter/commentary

## How To Turn a Project Into CV Evidence

Track:

- project title
- supervisor
- institution
- your role
- skills used
- outputs created
- presentation/submission/publication status
- dates
- evidence link
- referee/contact

CV phrasing examples:

- Assisted with literature review and data extraction for a retrospective audit of [topic].
- Co-authored abstract submitted to [conference].
- Presented findings at [department meeting/conference].
- Developed data collection tool for quality improvement project assessing [standard].
- Drafted background and discussion sections for a case report on [topic].

## What To Clarify Early

Before doing major work, ask:

- What output are we aiming for?
- Who is responsible for each section?
- What is the timeline?
- What are the authorship expectations?
- Where will this be submitted?
- What happens if the project stalls?

## Authorship Basics

Authorship should reflect meaningful contribution and follow journal/institutional expectations.

Do not assume:

- "I collected data" always equals authorship
- "I know the supervisor" means authorship
- "I started the project" means first author

Use the contribution tracker so your work is visible.

## Submission Targets

Possible targets:

- university research day
- hospital audit meeting
- specialty society conference
- student medical journal
- case report journal
- specialty conference
- local poster day

Pick the target early so the format does not surprise you later.

## Beginner Rule

Every active project should have one next output.

Examples:

- case report: submit abstract by July
- audit: present baseline data at department meeting
- review: complete first screening pass
- retrospective study: finalise data dictionary
