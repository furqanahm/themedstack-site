# AI Prompt Vault

Use these prompts to move faster, not to fake expertise. Always verify facts, references, ethics requirements, and clinical claims with real sources and your supervisor.

## Find a Focused Research Question

I am an Australian medical student interested in [specialty/topic]. I have [weekly time] available and [experience level]. Give me 10 realistic beginner research project ideas. For each idea, include project type, possible supervisor, likely data source, possible output, and biggest risk.

## Narrow a Broad Topic

Turn this broad topic into 10 narrower research questions suitable for a medical student: [topic]. Prefer questions that could become a case report, audit/QI project, literature review, or retrospective study.

## Supervisor Shortlist

I want to find supervisors in [specialty/location/university/hospital]. Create a search strategy for finding potential supervisors using PubMed, Google Scholar, hospital websites, university profiles, and specialty societies. Include what details I should record in a CRM.

## Tailor a Research Outreach Email

Draft a concise email to Dr [Surname], a [specialty] consultant/researcher. I am a [year] medical student at [university]. I am interested in [topic] because [reason]. I found their work on [paper/project]. I want to ask whether there are any research projects I could assist with. Tone: respectful, specific, not too long, Australian medical student style.

## Prepare for Supervisor Meeting

I have a meeting with a potential research supervisor about [topic]. Create a 15-minute meeting agenda, questions to ask, and a short script for introducing myself. Include questions about approvals, authorship, timeline, data access, and output plan.

## Literature Search Terms

For this research question: [question], create search term blocks using synonyms and related terms. Then create three PubMed-style search strings: broad, balanced, and narrow.

## Summarise Papers Into a Matrix

Create a literature matrix structure for papers on [topic]. Columns should include citation, study design, population, intervention/exposure, outcome, key finding, limitations, and how it informs my project.

## Project Rescue
