# ResearchStack Starter

ResearchStack Starter is a self-serve research launch system for Australian medical students who want to move from "I should do research" to a clear project pipeline, supervisor outreach plan, and first publishable output pathway.

This folder is the source pack. It can later be converted into PDFs, Notion pages, Google Sheets, Lemon Squeezy files, and website copy.

## Product Promise

In 30 days, the student should know:

- what type of research project suits their current stage
- how to find and contact potential supervisors
- how to track opportunities without losing follow-ups
- how to run a literature search without pretending they are already an academic
- how to turn a project into CV-ready evidence
- what to ask before joining a project
- what "audit/QI vs research" usually means in an Australian hospital context
- what their next concrete research action is this week

## Important Note

This product is education and organisation support only. It is not legal, ethics, university, hospital, or medical advice. Students should check their local university, hospital research office, supervisor, and HREC requirements before collecting data, contacting patients, submitting abstracts, or publishing.
