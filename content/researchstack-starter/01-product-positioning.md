# ResearchStack Starter: Product Positioning

## One-Line Offer

The beginner research system for Australian medical students who want to find projects, contact supervisors, track progress, and turn research into CV-ready outputs without guessing.

## Who It Is For

- Australian medical students in pre-clinical or clinical years
- Students who know research matters but do not know where to start
- Students aiming for competitive pathways where research, audits, presentations, publications, and leadership matter
- Students who have emailed one or two doctors before and heard nothing back
- Students who have joined a project but feel unclear about the next action

## Who It Is Not For

- PhD-level researchers
- Students who already have multiple first-author papers and a strong supervisor network
- Anyone looking for guaranteed publication
- Anyone wanting shortcuts around ethics, authorship, or academic integrity

## Core Outcome

By the end of the starter system, the student should have:

- a shortlist of realistic project types
- a list of 10-20 possible supervisors, teams, or project sources
- 3-5 tailored outreach emails ready to send
- a research pipeline tracker
- one project brief drafted
- one follow-up rhythm
- a CV evidence log for research outputs
- a clear understanding of what to ask before committing

## Product Levels

### ResearchStack Starter

Self-serve system. No video required at launch.

Includes:

- starter guide
- project type playbook
- outreach scripts
- literature search worksheet
- audit/QI guide
- case report and abstract templates
- trackers
- AI prompt vault

Target price:

- Launch: AUD 197-299
- Bundle with MedStack OS: AUD 199-249 for founding access
- Future with feedback/cohort: AUD 499-999

### ResearchStack Pro

Future higher-ticket offer.

Could include:

- recorded walkthroughs
- live office hours
- project idea review
- CV/research profile review
- supervisor email feedback
- abstract/manuscript feedback
- templates for specific specialties

## Why This Can Sell

The pain is specific:

- "I need research for my CV but do not know how to start."
- "I do not know who to email."
- "I do not know what counts as a good project."
- "I do not know how to follow up without annoying people."
- "I joined a project and now nothing is happening."
- "I have done work but do not know how to turn it into an abstract, poster, or CV point."

The product should not promise publication. It should promise structure, momentum, and fewer beginner mistakes.

## Main Sales Angles

### Angle 1: The Research Starting Problem

Most students are told research matters, but nobody gives them the operating system for finding, starting, and finishing projects.

### Angle 2: Supervisor Outreach

Students lose months because they send vague emails or wait for opportunities instead of building a pipeline.

### Angle 3: CV Evidence

Research only helps if it becomes traceable evidence: role, contribution, abstract, poster, presentation, manuscript, publication, referee, or lesson.

### Angle 4: Australia-Specific Context

Australian students need to understand audits, quality improvement, HREC, hospital governance, specialty CV expectations, and local conference opportunities.
