# Publication and Submission Pack

## Journal Targeting Checklist

Before choosing a journal:

- does it publish your article type?
- is it indexed and legitimate?
- does your supervisor know it?
- does it have fees?
- does it require a reporting checklist?
- what is the word count?
- how many tables/figures?
- what reference style?
- does it require patient consent wording?
- does it require ethics/governance statement?

Avoid:

- suspiciously fast acceptance
- vague editorial board
- aggressive email invitations
- unclear fees
- poor journal reputation

## Conference Targeting Checklist

Before submitting:

- abstract deadline
- word count
- structure
- eligibility
- student prize category
- poster/oral format
- author limit
- registration cost
- supervisor approval
- ethics/governance wording

## Cover Letter Template

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled "[TITLE]" for publication as a [ARTICLE TYPE] in [JOURNAL].

This manuscript addresses [PROBLEM] by [WHAT YOUR STUDY/CASE/REVIEW DOES]. We believe it is relevant to your readership because [WHY THIS JOURNAL'S AUDIENCE CARES].

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved this submission.

[Ethics/governance/consent sentence, if applicable.]

Thank you for considering our manuscript.

Kind regards,

[Corresponding author]

- final wording
- author list/order
- affiliations
- ethics/governance/consent statement
- target [conference/journal]
- any required changes before submission

The submission deadline is [date].

Kind regards,

[Name]

## Revision Response Structure

Use a table:

| Reviewer comment | Response | Change made | Manuscript location |
|---|---|---|---|

Response formula:

1. Thank reviewer
2. State what you changed
3. Point to section/line/table
4. If disagreeing, be polite and explain why

Example:

We thank the reviewer for this helpful suggestion. We have clarified the inclusion criteria in the Methods section and added the date range for data collection. This change appears in Methods, paragraph 2.

## Final File Checklist

- manuscript file
- title page if separate
- abstract if separate
- tables
- figures
- figure legends
- supplementary files
- cover letter
- reporting checklist
- ethics/consent statement
- author contribution statement
- conflict of interest statement
- funding statement
- acknowledgements
- references checked
